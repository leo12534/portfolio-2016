What Do i Do?

I create clean, unique, and creative designs for the web that push boundaries. I've worked on everything from video editing, animation and graphics, print to web design.

Whether it's HTML, CSS, Jquery or the Adobe Design Suite It’s important for me to create functional and easy to use designs that are great to look at but also aim to get your message accross in a creative way.

Like any designer, I'm very meticulous with my work. When I have down time, I'm always looking for ways to improve my website, and am more than open to suggestions!

TO INFINITY AND BEYOND!

Beyond the internet, I try to explore what the city has to offer. My blog serves as a hub where you can find my inspirations, ideas, nerdy stuff or doodles that i create or come across.

When I'm not on the computer I love to play with my dog, Buddy, go for hikes and play basketball. On rainy days, I love creative video games that inspire thought, checking out live music, and reading real life printed books (nothing against those ereaders, but there's just something about having the pages in my hands).
